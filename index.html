MHP Brain Teaser

Daily science, chess and Go puzzles, Sudoku and focus sounds

An Android app for curious people who like to think.

What is in it

  • Four science puzzles a day: physics, mathematics, biology and chemistry, each with a worked answer and a short lesson.
  • Daily chess and Go, if you want them: find the best move in a real chess position, or solve a life-and-death Go problem. Three levels of each, and you choose your own daily mix.
  • Sudoku: 200 puzzles in four levels, with notes, undo and hints that explain the move.
  • Focus sounds you can mix yourself: gamma beat, café, noise, rain, ocean, forest, birds, bonfire, stream, wind.
  • Tilt Maze: roll the ball through the maze by tilting your phone.
  • News: science, engineering, robotics, AI and good-news headlines, refreshed every hour.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone, and everything except News works offline. Read the privacy policy.
