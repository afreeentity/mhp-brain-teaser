MHP Brain Teaser

Daily science puzzles, focus sounds and a tilt maze

An Android app for curious people who like to think.

What is in it

  • Four science puzzles a day: physics, mathematics, biology and chemistry, each with a worked answer and a short lesson.
  • Focus sounds you can mix yourself: gamma beat, café, noise, rain, ocean, forest, birds, bonfire, stream.
  • Tilt Maze: roll the ball through the maze by tilting your phone.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone. Read the privacy policy.
