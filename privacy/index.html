Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

MHP Brain Teaser ("the app") is published by MHP Solutions ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else. The only things it downloads are a list of news headlines and a small file that says which version of the app is the newest, both described under "Internet access and links" below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following on your device only, so it can work between sessions:

  • which puzzles you have answered and whether you were right;
  • the days you used the app (for the day streak);
  • your Tilt Maze and Sudoku scores, and any unfinished Sudoku;
  • the last downloaded list of news headlines;
  • which daily puzzle subjects you chose;
  • the newest version number the app has seen, so it can tell you when an update exists;
  • your focus-sound settings and app settings.

This information never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time with "Reset progress" in the app's settings, by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Device sensors

The Tilt Maze game reads your device's motion sensors to move the ball. The readings are used only on the device while you play and are not recorded or sent anywhere.

Internet access and links

Everything except the News section works offline.

News. When you open the News section, the app downloads one file of headlines from our website (afreeentity.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages). The request contains nothing about you: no account, no identifier, no usage information. As with any website visit, the hosting company (GitHub) can see the internet address (IP address) the request comes from; we have no access to that information and do not receive any logs. The last list is kept on your device so it can be read offline.

New-version notice. At most twice a day the app downloads one small file from the same website. It contains only the number of the newest version of the app and a line of text about it. If your copy is older, the app shows a notice with a button that opens its Google Play page. The request contains nothing about you, and the same applies to it as to the News download above.

Links. Headlines open on their publisher's website, and lessons link to Wikipedia. If you tap one, it opens in your web browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies to the visit.

Children

The app is intended for people aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
